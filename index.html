MARCH – AUGUST 2026

Functional Water Irrigation System (Water Ionizer)

Community Service Project — Electrical Engineering

Overview

A community service project focused on implementing a functional-water irrigation system for siamese pumpkin farming at Kelompok Tani Al Musthafa, Kampung Cipulus, Desa Mandalasari, Cikancung, Kabupaten Bandung. The system uses a water ionizer to produce functional water for plant watering and periodic sanitation.

My Contribution

As a student team member, participated in project activities covering initial observation and discussion, system design, installation, testing, training and mentoring, and monitoring and evaluation with the partner.

Technical System

The proposed system combines a water ionizer, water tank, pump, piping, and sprinkler or drip irrigation. The water source can be supplied from PLTS or PLN, while electrolysis in the water ionizer produces alkaline and acidic water for different application purposes.

Technical Focus

Water Ionizer Irrigation Pump & Piping Appropriate Technology Energy Integration Field Implementation

Team & Collaboration

Conducted as a community service activity with lecturers and student members from Electrical Engineering, in collaboration with Kelompok Tani Al Musthafa as the partner.
